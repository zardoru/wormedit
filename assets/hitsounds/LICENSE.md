# CC0 default hitsounds

These twelve samples are original procedural sounds generated by
`scripts/generate-hitsounds.py` for wormedit. No osu! skin or third-party audio
was used. The generator fixes its random seeds and writes stereo 44.1 kHz PCM.

To the extent possible under law, the authors dedicate these generated samples
to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
