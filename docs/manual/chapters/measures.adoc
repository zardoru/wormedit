[#measures]
= Measures and bar lines

In Wormedit a bar line is an object you can select, place, move, copy and delete, much like a note.
Splitting a long measure or merging two short ones is a drawing operation, not a dialog.

Bar lines are about how the chart is _notated_. By default, editing them never moves a note in
time: notes keep playing at the same moment, and only the measure they are written in changes.

[#measure-lane]
== The measure lane

Turn on the measure lane with its button in the toolbar (_Show measure lane: select, place and
move bar lines_). The measure numbers at the left of the canvas become a lane with a handle on
each bar line.

* *Select*: click a handle. kbd:[Shift] adds to the selection and kbd:[Ctrl] toggles, as with
notes. An area selection that crosses the lane picks up the bar lines inside it. Selected handles
are gold.
* *Place*: with the Draw tool, click the lane to split the measure at the snapped row. The new bar
line is selected, and later measures are renumbered.
* *Delete*: select bar lines and press kbd:[Delete]. Each one merges its measure into the one
before it; notes stay where they are.

.A bar line placed with the Draw tool in the measure lane, selected (captured at {shot-measure-lane-revision})
image::measure-lane.png[The measure lane with a new gold bar line splitting a measure, and Bar line placed in the status bar]

Hiding the measure lane drops bar lines from the selection, so ordinary editing never moves them
by accident.

[#toggle-measure-line]
== Toggling a bar line from the keyboard

kbd:[Ctrl+M] (*Toggle Measure Line at Cursor*) works without the measure lane and without the
mouse:

* With the cursor _inside an authored measure_, it adds a bar line at the cursor, splitting the
measure there. The cursor ends up on the new bar line. In empty virtual space, place a note first
to materialize the measure; Ctrl+M cannot split virtual measures.
* With the cursor _on_ a bar line, it removes it, merging that measure into the one before it. The
cursor keeps its place in the music.

So kbd:[Ctrl+M] twice undoes itself, and combined with the snap and the cursor keys it reshapes
measures quickly: walk to the beat where a measure should end, press kbd:[Ctrl+M]; walk onto a bar
line that should not be there, press kbd:[Ctrl+M]. Notes never move in time, and each press is one
Undo step. The first bar line of the chart cannot be removed.

== Moving bar lines

Bar lines move with the rest of the selection. Select them together with notes (an area selection
does both) and drag one of the selected notes, or step-move the selection. A bar line handle is
not dragged directly.

While the measure lane is shown, a second toolbar button decides what moving a bar line does to the
music after it:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Setting |Effect

|Keep in place (default)
|_Moved bar lines keep other objects in place._ Only the measure boundaries change; every other
object keeps its time.

|Carry
|_Moved bar lines carry the content after them._ Everything after a moved bar line moves with it,
timing changes included.
|===

== Copying and pasting bar lines

Copy, cut and paste include selected bar lines. Pasting splits measures where the copied bar lines
fall, so a copied phrase brings its measure structure along; cutting merges the originals back.
Each is one Undo step, and no other object moves in time.

== Other measure commands

The command menu (kbd:[Ctrl+Shift+P]) has *Insert Measure Before*, *Insert Measure After* and
*Remove Empty Measure* for the measure at the cursor, and the inspector's *Measure at cursor*
section shows that measure's details.
