[#the-wormedit-way]
= The Wormedit way

Most chart editors make you choose. In a BMS editor you pick a sound, then click notes onto a grid
with the mouse. In the StepMania editor you move a cursor through the song with the arrow keys and
press a lane key to drop a note under it. Wormedit does both on one canvas, and the quickest way to
chart is to keep one hand on each: the keyboard for _where_ and _what_, the mouse for fixing,
selecting and drawing shapes.

== The charting loop

Once a chart is open in Compose (kbd:[F4]), almost all charting is this loop:

. *Pick the tool.* kbd:[Alt+2] selects the Draw tool. Lane keys only enter notes with the Draw
tool active (or while notes are selected, where they move them instead).
. *Arm a sound.* Double-click a sound in the Sounds dock, or press kbd:[Ctrl+K] and type part of
its name. The armed sound is outlined and marked _armed_; every note you place uses it.
. *Pick the snap.* kbd:[Left] and kbd:[Right] step through the snap list; the status bar shows
the current one.
. *Walk and play.* kbd:[Up] and kbd:[Down] move the cursor one snap. Press a lane key to place
the armed sound on that lane at the cursor; press it again on an existing note to remove it. Hold
a lane key while you move the cursor to draw a hold.
. *Listen.* kbd:[Space] plays from the cursor and stops. kbd:[Ctrl+Shift+Space] plays from the
start.
. *Fix with the mouse.* Drag notes to move them, drag across empty space to select, right-drag
with the Draw tool to erase. kbd:[Alt]-drag on a note to lengthen or shorten it: a tap you just
typed becomes a hold without retyping it.

.The charting loop: Draw tool, an armed sound, lane keys and kbd:[Up] between them (captured at {shot-keyboard-entry-revision})

The yellow line is the edit cursor. It stays still on the screen; the chart scrolls under it. Notes
you place with the mouse never move the cursor, so you can fix something higher up and keep typing
where you were.

[#speed-keys]
== Keys worth learning first

These are the keys that make the loop fast. The <<keyboard-reference,keyboard reference>> lists
every command.

[cols="2,3",options="header"]
|===
|Keys |What they do

|kbd:[Alt+1] / kbd:[Alt+2] / kbd:[Alt+3]
|Select, Draw and Layer tools.

|kbd:[Alt+4] … kbd:[Alt+8]
|Arm a special note kind: mine, invisible, lift, fake, roll. Only kinds the chart's format
supports are offered.

|kbd:[Ctrl+K]
|Go to sound: find a sound by name and arm it without touching the mouse.

|kbd:[Left] / kbd:[Right]
|Coarser or finer snap.

|kbd:[Up] / kbd:[Down]
|Move the cursor one snap. Holding repeats.

|kbd:[PgUp] / kbd:[PgDn] (RePág / AvPág)
|Skip a measure (at least two beats). On macOS, kbd:[Option+Up] and kbd:[Option+Down].

|kbd:[Tab] held
|Accelerate: while kbd:[Tab] is down, kbd:[Up]/kbd:[Down] and kbd:[Left]/kbd:[Right] move a whole
measure, and kbd:[PgUp]/kbd:[PgDn] move two. Keep holding to keep moving.

|kbd:[Alt]-drag a note
|Lengthen or shorten it (Select tool). With several notes selected, all of them change by the same
amount.

|kbd:[Ctrl+G]
|Go to measure by number.

|lane keys
|Place or remove the armed note at the cursor. Hold one while moving the cursor to make a hold.
With notes selected, move the selection to that lane instead.

|kbd:[Shift] + lane key
|Move the first selected note to that lane and deselect it; press again for the next one. This
reassigns a run of notes lane by lane without the mouse.

|kbd:[`]
|Move the selected notes to the BGM (background) lanes. kbd:[Shift+`] moves only the next one.

|kbd:[Ctrl+Shift+A]
|Select every note in the chart that uses the same sound as the selection.

|kbd:[Ctrl+1] … kbd:[Ctrl+9]
|Target a layer from the Layers dock; new notes are placed on it.

|kbd:[Space]
|Play from the cursor, or stop.
|===

TIP: Lane keys are physical keys: they stay in the same place on the keyboard whatever its
language layout. The defaults for each layout are in the <<layout-reference,layout reference>>.
