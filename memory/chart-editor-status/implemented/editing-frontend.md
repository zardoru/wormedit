The left rail labels those measures with source-facing numbers; clicking it selects even an empty virtual measure, highlights its number, and moves the visible cursor without materializing the measure. Step and measure-page navigation carry a virtual cursor through `#999` without materializing measures; the field scene draws it in the inherited lane. A tap click, a lane-key tap or hold on release, or armed Sample/BGM/BGA placement in an empty virtual BMS measure creates the intervening default geometry and event in one undoable command. Core tests cover the last measure, invalid targets, hold collisions, undo/redo, and scrolling without mutation.
